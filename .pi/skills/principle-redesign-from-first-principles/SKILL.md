# Redesign from First Principles

Understand the current design and its requirements.

Consider what design you would choose if the new requirement had existed from the beginning.

Compare that design with the existing implementation.
Do not preserve existing structures solely because they already exist.

Propagate the chosen design consistently through affected types, callers, tests and documentation.

Consider the redesign as a whole, but deliver it incrementally in verifiable units.

Keep the scope proportional to the requirement.
Do not redesign unrelated parts of the system.
