# Reports

Reports owns read-only analysis of rental commitments and equipment reservation demand, separately from financial analysis and operational management.

Access to a report includes the facts returned by that report, including rental amounts and invoice registrations where applicable. It does not imply access to rental, invoice, customer, or inventory management. Links and additional reads into those areas require their own permissions.

Periods are attributed by rental start in the tenant timezone, including future starts. These reports describe current commitments, not frozen historical statistics, equipment usage, collections, or balances. Invoice amounts are independent of accepted rental prices. Missing financial facts must remain missing rather than being replaced with zero or inferred values.
