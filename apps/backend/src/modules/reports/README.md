# Reports

Reports interprets current accepted commitments, not immutable historical activity. A report for a past period is not a frozen ledger or an accounting close.

Accepted rental amounts are commercial agreements, not evidence of collection. External invoice registrations are independent facts; differences between invoice and rental amounts do not establish balances or payment status.

Missing financial facts indicate incomplete persisted data, not zero amounts or permission to infer a currency or date. Preserve that uncertainty rather than inventing values or hiding affected commitments.

Equipment reservation metrics describe commitment demand, not actual use, possession, rental duration, or inventory utilization. Physical assignment and replacement are fulfillment decisions, not additional commercial demand.
