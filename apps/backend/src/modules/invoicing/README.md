# Invoicing

An invoice registration describes an invoice already issued outside Depiqo; it is not issuance or fiscal validation. Removing a mistaken registration does not cancel or delete the external invoice. Subsequent rental cancellation does not invalidate an invoice already issued externally.

Invoice amounts are deliberately independent of accepted rental prices. A difference between those amounts is not an inconsistency, an unpaid balance, or evidence of incomplete invoicing. Rental Commitment remains authoritative for accepted revenue and third-party owner splits; invoice registrations are not a replacement financial source of truth.
