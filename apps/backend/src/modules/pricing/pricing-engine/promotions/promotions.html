Promotion Engine

Use a promotion to reduce a proposed rental price. A promotion says when it can apply, what it can discount, and whether another promotion can follow it.

Resolve participating lines

Scope and exclusions

Scope determines the candidate participating lines. Exclusions remove lines from that scope. Conditions are evaluated from the remaining eligible lines, and the effect is calculated from and allocated only to those lines.

Core rule: A promotion can qualify from and financially affect only the lines inside its resolved scope after exclusions. For a whole-order promotion, use an all-lines scope with no exclusions.

Follow one calculation

An order has a camera for 100 and a tripod for 50. A 20% promotion is scoped to cameras. A 10% promotion uses an all-lines scope with no exclusions.

  1. The camera promotion reduces the camera from 100 to 80.
  2. The all-lines promotion calculates from the new total: 130, not 150.
  3. Its 13 discount is allocated across both current line totals.

Each new discount uses the current totals. No line and no order total can become negative.

How the engine works

Price the base rental lines
Find eligible automatic promotions
Validate the submitted coupon and its promotion
Sort all candidates by highest priority
Apply discounts in order and stop after a non-stackable one
Save line adjustments and the final promotion breakdown

Dates are inclusive local dates. A promotion must be active, belong to the tenant, meet its conditions, and match at least one non-excluded line.

Know the limits

The engine supports percentage and fixed discounts, scopes, exclusions, order thresholds, rental-duration limits, coupons, priorities, and stacking.

It does not support buy-X-get-Y, free units, bundles, customer segments on promotions, tiered campaign rules, or arbitrary formulas. Add these as explicit domain behavior, not JSON rules.

Read the result

The result records every line adjustment. It also records each applied promotion and the applied coupon. Consumers can preserve this breakdown when a rental price becomes historical truth.
